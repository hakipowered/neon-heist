How to play

Move: drag on the left side of the screen. Aim: drag on the right side. You can also drag on the FIRE button to aim while shooting.

Survivors: pick one of four, each with its own gun and three abilities. Vanguard (rifle, frag, dash, Overdrive), Breacher (scattergun, concussion blast, jet-jump slam, Iron Skin), Marksman (piercing rail rifle, shock mines, cloak, Lance) and Demolisher (grenade launcher, cluster bomb, rocket jump, missile barrage). The special charges as you deal damage.

Items: spend scrap ⬢ on crates around the arena to get random items. They stack: white is Common, green Uncommon, red Legendary. Between waves the Armory sells one of three items, and you can reroll or repair. Kills also give XP, and each level adds damage and health.

Power-ups: robots sometimes drop RAGE (double damage), HASTE (speed and fire rate) or AEGIS (half damage and healing) for 10 seconds.

Robots: Troopers flank you and fall back to cover. Snipers paint you with a red laser first, so break line of sight. Bulwarks have a front shield, so aim for the head or hit them from the side. Rushers beep before they blow. Mortar tanks mark where shells land. From wave 6, gold elites are tougher.

Bosses (every 5th wave): the Titan (shoot the head, jump its laser and shockwaves) and the Siege Walker (hit the red dome on top, dodge the mortar rain). They take turns.

Between waves: the next wave starts when both players press READY. If you go down, your partner revives you by standing next to you.

Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, mouse to aim (click to lock), click to fire, Space to jump (double jump with boosters), G or right-click for ability 1, Shift for ability 2, F for the special, E to open crates, B for the Armory, R for ready.
