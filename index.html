How to play

Move: drag on the left side of the screen. Aim: drag on the right side. You can also drag on the FIRE button to aim while shooting.

Robots: Troopers flank you in squads and fall back to cover when hurt. Snipers paint you with a red laser before they fire, so break line of sight. Bulwarks carry a front shield: hit them from the side or behind, or use explosives. Rushers flash and beep before they blow, so back off. Mortar tanks mark where shells will land. From wave 6, gold elites are tougher and pay double.

Titan (every 5th wave): its head is a weak point. Hurt it and it starts sweeping a laser (jump over it or hide behind cover), then slams shockwaves along the ground (jump them).

Your abilities: DASH is a quick dodge that shots pass through. NADE throws a bouncing grenade. Overdrive charges as you deal damage: double damage, faster fire, infinite ammo and half damage taken for 8 seconds. Headshots deal double damage. Quick kills build a streak that multiplies your scrap. Red barrels explode.

Scrap ⬢: earned per kill and per wave cleared. Between waves, spend it on damage, fire rate, health, armor, speed, and new weapons: shotgun, rail gun and rocket launcher.

Down, not out: at zero health you go down. Your partner revives you by standing next to you for 3 seconds. If you're both down, it's game over.

Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, mouse to aim (click to lock), click to fire, Space to jump, Shift to dash, G or right-click for a grenade, F for Overdrive, Q to swap, B for upgrades.
