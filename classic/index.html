How to play

Move: touch anywhere and drag. A joystick appears under your thumb.

Dash: the pink button. You hold 2 charges that refill over time.

Ram: dash into your rival to steal a quarter of their cores. One goes straight to you, the rest scatter.

Cores are worth 1. Prisms are worth 5 and vanish fast.

Pickups: 🛡 Shield blocks one ram and stuns the attacker. ⚡ Overdrive makes you faster and refills dashes quicker. 🧲 Magnet pulls nearby cores in.

Black hole: opens at 35s left. It pulls you in, and touching it costs a third of your cores.

Last 15s: everything is worth double. Win 2 rounds to take the match.
